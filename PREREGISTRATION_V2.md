## 1. Primary research question

Same question as v1 §1, asked on a broader, mechanically defined cross-section:

Does the prospectively observed combination of qualitative investment signals, company fundamentals and valuation contain information about subsequent benchmark-relative total returns that is not captured by simpler financial or momentum baselines?

A negative result is a valid outcome and must be reported as such.

## 2. Purpose of v2 relative to v1

v2 exists to increase the number of distinct companies and the cross-sectional dispersion of scores within each weekly cohort. It does not exist to reach a nominal observation count faster. Weekly observations of the same company overlap and are not independent; v2 inference is therefore designed around the cross-section of each cohort (section 10).

## 4. Observation unit and scoring model

The observation unit is a ticker within an official frozen weekly snapshot of `data/weekly_signals.json`, generated by the unchanged scheduled pipeline.

The score is the unchanged `weekly-v1.0` Signal Score (equal-weight mean of fundamental_strength, novelty, pricing_headroom, valuation and 100 - execution_risk; `docs/WEEKLY_SIGNALS.md`). v2 tests the same model on a broader universe; it introduces no new scoring formula. Any change to the formula requires a new model version evaluated prospectively (v1 §8).

The qualitative components (novelty, pricing_headroom, execution_risk) must be produced under `docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL_V2.md`. An entry not produced under that protocol makes the component non-eligible for v2.

## 5. Eligibility and missing data

An observation is v2-eligible when:

1. the snapshot was captured after the v2 activation record (section 15);
2. all five components are present, so the Signal Score is not null;
3. each qualitative component carries the research-protocol provenance fields required by `docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL_V2.md`;
4. a T0 anchor can be created under the v1 market conventions (section 6).

Incomplete observations stay visible in the ledger and are counted with their missing components; they are never imputed. Tickers that are structurally incomplete under `weekly-v1.0` (on 2026-09-29: NVO, valuation missing because reporting and trading currencies differ; SYM, fundamental_strength missing) remain in the universe and are reported every month.

## 6. Market data, T0 and returns

v2 inherits, without modification, the v1 market conventions: `market_pit` as the source of truth, immutability and no vendor repair (A1-A3), eligible sessions (A4), total-return convention (A7), horizon measurement date (A8), completed-session and boundary rules (B1), corporate actions (B3), maturity bounds and gap rule (B7), contiguous collection (C1-C4).

The only extensions are:

- market collection covers the 23 v2 tickers that are not in v1;
- each v2 snapshot freezes a `t0_after_session` boundary for every v2-eligible ticker;
- **isolation:** a failure, staleness or gap on a v2-only ticker must never block, delay or change the v1 collection, the v1 15-series completeness rule (B2) or v1 outcomes. v2-only series have their own completeness check.

A ticker whose market collection has not yet started cannot anchor T0; it is reported as `unavailable` for that snapshot, not backfilled.

## 9. Score buckets and diagnostic definitions

Unchanged from v1: buckets 0-39, 40-59, 60-79, 80-100, never merged after seeing results; false positive = score >= 80 and M+6 excess <= 0; false negative = score < 40 and M+6 excess > 0.

## 10. Primary analysis

N always means the number of v2-eligible observations with a written outcome at the horizon considered, reported together with the number of distinct tickers and of weekly cohorts.

Primary analysis at M+6:

1. **Per-cohort cross-section.** For each weekly cohort with at least 10 eligible observations, compute the Spearman rank correlation between Signal Score and M+6 excess return.
2. **Summary.** Report the mean and median of the per-cohort coefficients and their full distribution over time.
3. **Dependence-aware uncertainty.** Consecutive cohorts overlap by up to 25 of 26 weeks. Uncertainty is computed by a moving-block bootstrap over cohorts with block length 26 weeks. A non-overlapping subsample (the first eligible cohort, then every 26th cohort) is also reported.
4. **Descriptive tables** as in v1 §11: N, median and mean excess return by bucket, hit rate by bucket, per ticker and per sector.

## 11. Baselines and ablation

Same three baselines as v1 §12, computed on the same v2 observations:

- A, financial only (fundamental_strength + valuation): eligible from activation;
- B, 12-1 month momentum: eligible for a ticker only once 12 months of its prospectively frozen `market_pit` history exist (A9, C3). For the 23 v2-only tickers this depends on the start date of their collection;
- C, FCF yield where valid at T0.

No baseline is dropped because it outperforms the full model.
