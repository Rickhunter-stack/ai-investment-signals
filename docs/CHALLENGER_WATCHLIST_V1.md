# Challenger Watchlist v1

This annex records companies discovered through the Unified Daily Brief that merit prospective follow-up. Inclusion is not an investment recommendation and does not modify weekly scores.

## Lifecycle
- DISCOVERED: credible new candidate with an explicit reason to monitor.
- WATCH: subsequent independent material evidence or enough evidence for recurring monitoring.
- DEEP_DIVE: accumulated evidence justifies dedicated sourced research.

Promotion is prospective only. A price decline alone does not qualify a company. Adoption evidence and valuation/pricing remain separate dimensions.

## Daily and weekly interaction
The daily brief may add a candidate as DISCOVERED after a material event. The weekly workflow reviews candidates for new evidence and may promote status prospectively. The watchlist must never rewrite frozen brief events or weekly snapshots and must not feed Signal Score automatically.

A DEEP_DIVE may later populate sourced qualitative research through the normal research process. Private companies may use ticker=null; never invent a ticker.
