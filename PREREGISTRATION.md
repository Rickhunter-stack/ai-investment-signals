# AI Investment Signals - Prospective Experiment Preregistration v1.0

**Preregistered:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** prospective protocol, to be frozen before outcome analysis  
**Repository:** `Rickhunter-stack/ai-investment-signals`

## 1. Primary research question

Does the prospectively observed combination of qualitative investment signals, company fundamentals and valuation contain information about subsequent benchmark-relative total returns that is not captured by simpler financial or momentum baselines?

This protocol must be able to produce a negative result. Failure to demonstrate incremental predictive value is a valid outcome.

## 2. Experimental universe

The v1 confirmatory universe is frozen to:

`NVDA, AVGO, QCOM, MU, GOOGL, AMZN, ADI, MDT, ISRG, GH`.

No security may be added to or removed from the v1 confirmatory sample because of subsequent performance. Delistings, acquisitions or other unavoidable corporate events remain observations and must be documented rather than silently removed.

Additional securities may later enter an explicitly separate exploratory universe, but they must not be mixed retrospectively into v1 confirmatory results.

## 3. Observation unit

The primary scored observation is the official frozen weekly ticker snapshot generated by the scheduled pipeline. Daily events are evidence inputs, not independent return observations unless a future protocol version explicitly preregisters an event-level experiment.

Only snapshots created prospectively under the repository's integrity rules qualify for confirmatory v1 analysis.

## 4. Information set and T0

### 4.1 Information cutoff

A weekly snapshot may use only information publicly available before its `as_of` cutoff and captured by the research pipeline without future leakage.

### 4.2 T0 return price convention

To avoid intraday timestamp ambiguity and unrealistic execution assumptions, v1 uses a conservative daily-close convention:

- if the frozen weekly snapshot is created after the relevant US regular-market close and that trading day's adjusted close is already available in `market_pit`, T0 is that trading day's immutable adjusted close;
- otherwise T0 is the next US trading day's immutable adjusted close after the snapshot becomes available;
- no intraday price is substituted retrospectively;
- the same calendar/trading-day rule is applied to the assigned benchmark.

This convention applies to the weekly confirmatory experiment. Daily event timestamps remain preserved for future event-level research but do not change v1 T0.

## 5. Price and return convention

The experimental market-data source is `market_pit`, not the legacy `market` table.

Security and benchmark returns use the same immutable `adjusted_close` convention. Once first stored for a ticker/date, a market observation is not overwritten by later vendor revisions.

For horizon H:

`absolute_return(H) = adjusted_close_H / adjusted_close_T0 - 1`

`excess_return(H) = security_return(H) - benchmark_return(H)`

If the exact target calendar date is not a trading day, use the first common US trading day on or after the target date for both security and benchmark. If a corporate event prevents a valid comparable observation, flag the outcome rather than silently imputing it.

## 6. Horizons

Frozen horizons are:

- M+1 = one calendar month after T0
- M+3 = three calendar months after T0
- M+6 = six calendar months after T0
- M+12 = twelve calendar months after T0

### Primary horizon

**M+6 is the primary confirmatory horizon.**

Rationale fixed prospectively: the framework targets industrial/fundamental signals that may require more than a few weeks to be reflected in fundamentals and valuation, while M+12 materially reduces the number of independent observations available during the early experiment.

M+1, M+3 and M+12 are secondary horizons and must not replace M+6 merely because they produce more favorable results.

## 7. Benchmark rule

Benchmark assignment is frozen prospectively by economic exposure, not selected after observing performance.

### QQQ

Use QQQ for technology / semiconductor / hyperscaler names:

- NVDA
- AVGO
- QCOM
- MU
- GOOGL
- AMZN
- ADI

### SPY

Use SPY for healthcare names:

- MDT

Both SPY and QQQ remain stored prospectively in `market_pit`.

A secondary sensitivity analysis may report every security against SPY and every security against QQQ, but the mapping above is the confirmatory benchmark and cannot be changed retrospectively.

## 8. Model versions

Historical observations retain the model version that generated them.

`weekly-v1.0` remains immutable. Any material change in fields, transformations, weights or scoring rules requires a new version.

A new model version is evaluated prospectively from its activation date. Retrospective application is allowed only as explicitly labeled exploratory backtesting and must never replace the genuine prospective history.

## 9. Current weekly-v1.0 signal model

The current model combines five dimensions:

1. fundamental strength
2. novelty
3. pricing headroom / degree not already priced
4. valuation
5. execution risk

Execution risk is adverse and therefore must be directionally transformed in the composite score. Current v1 weighting remains the repository's existing equal-weight baseline unless a future version is preregistered before use.

Missing components are not imputed merely to produce a score. An incomplete snapshot remains incomplete and is excluded from score-stratified confirmatory tests that require the missing value, while remaining visible in the historical ledger.

## 10. Primary endpoint

The primary endpoint is **M+6 benchmark-relative total return (excess return)** for eligible frozen weekly observations.

The primary descriptive relationship is whether higher prospectively frozen Signal Scores are associated with higher subsequent M+6 excess returns.

Because repeated weekly observations of the same security overlap in time and are not statistically independent, naive observation-level p-values must not be interpreted as confirmatory evidence.

## 11. Primary analysis

Until sample size supports more advanced inference, report:

- number of eligible observations;
- median M+6 excess return by prespecified Signal Score bucket;
- mean M+6 excess return by bucket;
- hit rate (`excess_return > 0`) by bucket;
- Spearman rank correlation between initial Signal Score and M+6 excess return;
- results by ticker and sector to expose concentration.

Prespecified score buckets:

- 0-39
- 40-59
- 60-79
- 80-100

Empty or very small buckets remain reported as such and must not be merged after seeing results solely to improve significance.

## 12. Baselines and ablation

The central claim concerns incremental value. Therefore the full framework must eventually be compared with simpler alternatives using the same T0 observations and horizons.

Prespecified baselines:

### Baseline A - financial only

Fundamental strength + valuation, using the contemporaneous components available in the frozen weekly snapshot.

### Baseline B - momentum

12-1 month price momentum, calculated using only immutable market observations available at T0. This baseline becomes eligible only after sufficient `market_pit` history exists prospectively; earlier observations are not reconstructed with later-downloaded history for the confirmatory sample.

### Baseline C - valuation

FCF yield when valid and available for the company at T0. Sector limitations must be reported rather than hidden.

The principal incremental question is whether the full Signal Score provides useful discrimination beyond these simpler baselines. No baseline is to be dropped because it outperforms the full model.

## 13. Qualitative interpretation

`FACT`, `WEAK_SIGNAL` and `HYPOTHESIS` remain distinct. LLM-derived judgments such as novelty, importance, confidence, pricing status and execution risk are treated as auditable research judgments, not objective measurements.

Future versions should strengthen scoring anchors and source-quality rules, but historical classifications are not rewritten when those rules improve.

## 14. Stories, theses and criticality

Story relationships, investment theses and AI Infrastructure Criticality are currently research-development layers, not part of the v1 primary confirmatory endpoint unless explicitly incorporated by a later preregistered model version.

Industrial criticality should represent relatively structural characteristics such as substitutability, supplier concentration, qualification time, switching cost and capacity lead time. Dynamic research-signal intensity should remain separate to avoid circularity.

## 15. Missing data

No future data may be used to fill a field that was missing at T0.

Rules:

- missing score component: preserve as missing;
- missing valid T0 market observation: defer to the next valid observation under section 4, otherwise flag unavailable;
- missing horizon price: keep outcome pending/unavailable;
- missing fundamental: do not backfill the historical score with a later filing.

Missingness counts must be reported with results.

## 16. Corporate actions and exceptional events

Adjusted prices are used consistently. Acquisitions, delistings, ticker changes, spin-offs and other events that may make a simple adjusted-price return misleading must be explicitly flagged and reviewed under a documented mechanical rule before inclusion in confirmatory aggregate statistics.

No observation may be excluded simply because its return is inconvenient.

## 17. False positives and false negatives

For descriptive diagnostics, a prespecified high-conviction observation is a complete Signal Score >= 80 and a low-conviction observation is < 40.

- **false positive:** Signal Score >= 80 and M+6 excess return <= 0;
- **false negative:** Signal Score < 40 and M+6 excess return > 0.

These are diagnostic definitions, not proof that the investment thesis caused the return. Middle-score observations are not forced into this binary classification.

## 18. Multiple testing and exploratory analyses

M+6 excess return is the primary outcome. All other horizons, subgroup analyses, thesis analyses, alternative benchmarks, alternative score thresholds, criticality analyses and future dashboard correlations are secondary or exploratory unless a later prospective preregistration explicitly promotes them before their outcomes are known.

Exploratory findings must be labeled exploratory. They may generate a new prospective hypothesis but may not be presented as if they had been preregistered.

## 19. Sample size and statistical restraint

No fixed minimum sample size is claimed to magically establish validity. The project will always report raw N and dependence structure.

Before approximately 100 eligible weekly ticker-observations and meaningful representation across several securities, results should be treated primarily as descriptive. Even beyond that point, overlapping horizons and repeated observations require dependence-aware inference (for example block/bootstrap or clustered methods) before strong statistical claims.

Economic magnitude, consistency, robustness and incremental performance versus baselines matter more than an isolated p-value.

## 20. Success and failure criteria

### Evidence supportive of the framework

Support requires a coherent pattern, not one winning stock. At minimum, the full model should show at M+6:

- monotonic or broadly ordered performance across score buckets;
- positive excess-return tendency in higher-score observations;
- positive rank association between score and future excess return;
- behavior not entirely explained by one ticker or one sector;
- incremental discrimination relative to the prespecified financial-only and momentum/valuation baselines when those baselines become eligible.

These criteria are intentionally not converted into a single arbitrary p-value threshold in v1.

### Evidence against incremental predictive value

The framework will be considered unsupported by the accumulated v1 evidence if, after a sufficiently populated prospective sample, scores show no stable relationship with M+6 excess returns, high-score observations do not outperform lower-score observations in a reasonably consistent way, or the full model provides no useful discrimination beyond the simpler prespecified baselines.

A negative conclusion must be preserved and reported rather than repaired by retrospective reweighting.

## 21. Universe and protocol changes

Any future change to:

- universe;
- primary horizon;
- benchmark mapping;
- T0 convention;
- primary endpoint;
- score construction;
- inclusion/exclusion rules;
- success criteria

requires a new dated protocol/model version before the affected future observations are generated.

The v1 protocol remains in Git history unchanged for auditability.

## 22. Selection-bias limitation

The human daily brief selects five high-value items and therefore contains an editorial selection mechanism. It must not be assumed to represent the complete information environment.

The planned investment scanner should eventually monitor the frozen experimental universe independently of the five-item human brief and may emit zero, one or many material events. Until then, conclusions about event frequency or absence of signals must acknowledge this selection bias.

## 23. Data-source limitations

`yfinance` is a practical collection source, not an institutional point-in-time feed. `market_pit` freezes the first value observed by this project and prevents later overwrites, but adjusted historical data may still contain vendor/corporate-action knowledge unavailable in the original past.

Fundamental point-in-time integrity is not yet fully solved. Confirmatory claims involving fundamentals must therefore wait for the dedicated point-in-time fundamental layer or explicitly acknowledge the limitation.

## 24. Planned implementation sequence after this preregistration

1. point-in-time fundamental ledger;
2. deterministic outcome engine for M+1/M+3/M+6/M+12;
3. baseline and ablation calculations;
4. investment-scanner separation from the five-item personal brief;
5. story classifier v2;
6. thesis layer;
7. structural AI Infrastructure Criticality;
8. divergence detection and richer dashboard views.

This order may change for engineering reasons, but no later implementation may silently alter the confirmatory definitions above.

## 25. Final rule

The project exists to test a hypothesis, not to defend it.

**Frozen historical observations, inconvenient outcomes, failed predictions and negative results are part of the dataset and must remain visible.**
